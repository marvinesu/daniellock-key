# Legacy source comparison

- Legacy source: `https://danielslockkey.com/`
- Rebuilt host: `https://daniels.webxni.com/`
- Reviewed: 2026-08-19

## What the legacy site did well

- Made Daniel personally central with the direct promise to call the owner-operator.
- Explained non-destructive entry, quoting before work, and a simple four-step service path.
- Used project imagery, service explanations, FAQs, and direct call/request actions.

## What was not carried forward

- The apartment/street address and radius marker, because this is a mobile service-area business.
- Unsupported after-hours, holiday, price, arrival, experience, and broad automotive-key claims.
- Replacement keys, remotes, cutting, programming, transponders, and ignition work.
- Generic testimonials or service claims without attributable evidence.

## Rebuilt structure and improvements

| Legacy pattern | Rebuilt implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Daniel story inside one long page | Owner-led homepage, dedicated About route, direct Call Daniel language, and educational resource pages |
| Mixed residential/vehicle service list | Approved home, rental, selected commercial, repair, rekeying, turnover, and passenger-vehicle entry paths |
| One contact point | Persistent phone action plus a consent-based scheduled-work form and honest delivery states |
| Project image strip | Responsive Hollywood-focused hero media and seven service-specific scenes used throughout the route system |
| Address-radius coverage | Leaflet/OpenStreetMap service-area context and qualified Central Los Angeles location pages |
| Thin page navigation | Services, Residential, Commercial, Areas, Resources, About, FAQ, and Contact in the persistent header |

## Navigation and conversion check

- Daniel remains the visible differentiator in the notice bar, hero, call controls, process, About page, and footer.
- The California LCO 8506 claim is published only from the reviewed DCA/BSIS record and without the address of record.
- Calls remain the urgent path; the website form is positioned for scheduled work and does not request credentials or identity documents.
- Recovered social profiles are presented as accessible icon links.

## Outstanding owner inputs

- Confirm any biography, business-history, review, or operating-hours claims before publication.
- Do not restore automotive key/fob/programming content unless that capability is explicitly verified and approved.
